EDF5_MultiSlotMod v0.6.80 (Users)
Rooms of 5 to 8 players in EARTH DEFENSE FORCE 5.
Pre-release: 8 players is the mod's target; not every mission has been tested
with 8.

Requirements
- EDFModLoader 1.0.10 installed in the game (not included in this ZIP):
  https://github.com/BlueAmulet/EDFModLoader
- Every player in the room on this same version and configuration.

Installation
1. Close the game.
2. Right-click the downloaded ZIP, Properties, tick "Unblock" and press OK.
   Without this, Windows may stop the mod from loading.
3. In Mods\Plugins, delete older versions if present:
   EDF5TrafficSniffer.dll, EDF5_MultiSlotMod.dll and EDF5_MultiSlotMod.ini.
4. Extract the ZIP into the game folder (where EDF5.exe is) and merge the Mods
   folder.
5. The title screen should show EDF5_MultiSlotMod v0.6.80 in the top-right
   corner. Creating or joining a room shows the same version in the chat.

If the version does not appear, the mod did not load or disabled itself for
safety. Check step 2 and that the game is on the current Steam version. On
Windows 11, Smart App Control can also block the DLL.

Keys
- F4: invite friends through Steam in a room you created, even with 4 or more
  players.
- Mouse wheel: scroll the room's player list.
- Tab: show or hide your stats panel during a mission.
- Home: on screens wider than 16:9, switch between the wide and the original
  picture during a mission.

Ultrawide screens (21:9, 32:9)
During missions the picture fills the screen without stretching and the HUD
moves to the corners. Title, menus and room keep the original picture. 16:9 and
16:10 screens are unchanged.

Configuration
Mods\Plugins\EDF5_MultiSlotMod.ini explains each option.

This build writes no logs or crash reports.

To uninstall, delete EDF5_MultiSlotMod.dll and EDF5_MultiSlotMod.ini from
Mods\Plugins.
